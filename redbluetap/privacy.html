Privacy Policy

This policy explains how information is handled when you play Red Blue Tap, use its reminder and privacy controls, visit this website, or contact KiteMosaic.

At a glance

  • You do not need a Red Blue Tap account. The game saves progress, results and preferences locally.
  • KiteMosaic does not operate a server that receives your saved game database or reminder-timing history.
  • Google AdMob and Google's consent services can process information from your device for advertising and related purposes. This is data processing through the app, even when KiteMosaic does not directly receive the underlying information.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send.

Information kept on your device

The game stores information in its Android application storage so that it can work between visits:

  • Game records: unlocked pictures, completion results, attempts, mistakes, reaction-time statistics and any earlier play history retained by an app update.
  • Preferences: appearance, music, selected mystery, tutorial state and other saved game choices.
  • Reminder information: your chosen delivery window and quiet hours, the next clue in the reminder sequence, delivery records used to avoid duplicates, and a limited history of in-app interaction times used to choose a suitable reminder time.
  • Privacy choices: consent status and choices stored by Google's consent software.

KiteMosaic does not upload your saved picture results, individual attempt-history database or locally learned reminder schedule to its own servers or supply those records to AdMob for ad targeting. Advertising software can independently observe usage and technical events as described below; keeping the game database local does not prevent that separate processing.

The game does not ask you to enter a name, email address or date of birth to play. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photo library or Android location. It has no player chat, public profiles or account-based cloud save.

Google advertising services

Red Blue Tap includes Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to handle applicable privacy messages and choices. These services communicate with Google directly from your device. The game does not require a KiteMosaic account for this to happen.

Google's advertising SDK disclosure identifies the following categories. What is available in a particular request depends on device settings, consent, SDK configuration and applicable restrictions:

InformationExamples and purpose
Approximate locationYour IP address can indicate a broad location. Red Blue Tap does not request GPS or Android location coordinates.
App interactionsUsage events, such as opening the app, taps and interactions with advertising, including video views where applicable.
DiagnosticsTechnical and performance information about the app and advertising SDK, such as startup speed, unresponsiveness and energy use.
Device or account identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts. Availability and permitted uses vary.

Google describes advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as the SDK's purposes. These include delivering and measuring ads and protecting advertising services against misuse. App set ID is restricted to essential non-advertising uses such as analytics and fraud prevention. KiteMosaic does not use it for ad personalisation or ad measurement, or link it to other identifiers or personal data for advertising.

Google and advertising partners involved in serving an ad may handle information under their own privacy terms and the applicable choices. Provider information can be reviewed in the Google privacy message where shown. A request may also involve cookies or similar local-storage technologies, subject to the relevant consent and advertising treatment.

Read Google's advertising SDK data disclosure, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google's Privacy Policy. Integrating these services does not remove KiteMosaic's responsibility to explain their use in Red Blue Tap.

Consent and advertising choices

Where applicable, the game presents Google's privacy message. UMP checks which message and privacy options are required, stores your selections and provides choice signals used by the advertising integration. Its communication with Google is separate from the locally stored game records.

You can review or change advertising choices through Settings → Privacy Choices when that option is available. Options can differ by region and include choices about personalised advertising, processing purposes or providers. Consent can be withdrawn through the available privacy controls; withdrawing it does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Declining personalised ads does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing. Depending on your choices and applicable rules, limited technical information may still be processed to deliver ads, measure permitted activity or prevent fraud. Google explains the differences between personalised and non-personalised ads and limited ads.

You can also use Android's advertising privacy controls, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID where supported, and Google's My Ad Center. These controls have different scopes: changing one does not erase every identifier or information already processed by a provider.

Advertising consent and Android notification permission are separate choices. Declining notifications does not prevent you from playing. The installed puzzles and local progress work without an internet connection; advertising and consent updates require a connection.

Mystery Reminders

If Android permits notifications, Red Blue Tap can post a locally scheduled daily clue reminder. You can choose a delivery window and quiet hours in the game. Notification delivery is controlled by the app's notification permission and channel in Android Settings, where you can turn it off at any time.

To help choose a time within your selected window, the game can use recent times when you opened Red Blue Tap, started gameplay or opened one of its reminders. This limited timing information stays on your device. It is not a record of your activity in other apps and is not sent to a KiteMosaic server or used by KiteMosaic for ad targeting.

The reminder feature does not read your device-wide screen-time history, monitor your internet connection to infer sleep, or access your location. Clues come from the app's bundled picture library. Your Android lock-screen settings determine whether a reminder is visible while the phone is locked.

Support and developer reports

If you contact kitemosaic@gmail.com, KiteMosaic receives the address you write from, your message and any information or attachments you provide. We use them to respond, investigate the issue, handle privacy requests and keep necessary correspondence. Sending an email is optional; include only the information needed for your request. Our mailbox is provided by Google Gmail.

AdMob provides developer reports, such as advertising impressions, clicks and estimated earnings. KiteMosaic may use these aggregate reports to understand advertising performance. They are separate from the saved progress and individual attempt-history database on your phone.

Google Play may also make store and technical reports available to developers, including install statistics and crash or performance information under Google's own collection and sharing settings. If you post a public store review, its text and public profile information can be visible to KiteMosaic and other people. These platform features are governed by Google's terms and privacy information.

Who handles information

  • KiteMosaic: support correspondence, privacy requests and reports made available through the developer services described above.
  • Google and applicable advertising partners: advertising, consent handling, related measurement, security and fraud prevention, as explained in their privacy information and the applicable privacy message.
  • Google Gmail: delivery and storage of messages sent to our contact address.
  • GitHub: hosting and protecting this website, as explained below.

If required by law, KiteMosaic may disclose information it actually holds in response to a valid legal request. We do not have a remote copy of your local game records to provide. We do not publish private support messages as part of the game.

Retention and deletion

Game data on your device

Saved progress and preferences remain until you remove them. An in-app history-deletion control removes the records identified by that control; it does not necessarily reset every picture result, preference or consent choice.

To reset the installation's saved data, use Android Settings → Apps → Red Blue Tap → Storage → Clear storage or the equivalent option on your device. Uninstalling also removes the app's local storage. Clearing the cache is not the same as clearing saved data. Device backup, transfer or restoration services, where applicable, have their own controls and can affect whether a copy is restored.

Clearing storage resets your local progress, settings and reminder history. The app may ask for setup or privacy choices again. KiteMosaic cannot remotely read, recover or delete records kept only on your phone.

Support information

We retain correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request, handle related follow-up, maintain necessary records of privacy requests, or meet applicable legal obligations and disputes. When those reasons no longer apply, we delete the correspondence or remove identifying details. You can ask us to delete information you have sent, subject to any lawful need to retain it.

Google and other providers

Provider-held data follows that provider's retention practices, which can differ by data type, purpose and legal requirements. See Google's retention explanation and Google Account privacy controls for available options. GitHub describes its retention in its Privacy Statement.

Deleting local app data, uninstalling, changing consent or resetting an advertising ID does not automatically delete data already held by Google or another provider. There is no Red Blue Tap account to close.

Security and international processing

Local game records use Android's application storage protections. Google states that information collected by its advertising SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. This website is served over HTTPS. These protections do not mean that all local records have additional app-level encryption or that any device or online service is immune to unauthorised access.

Google and GitHub operate internationally. Advertising data, support email and website request information may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Their applicable safeguards and transfer mechanisms are described in Google's data transfer information and GitHub's international transfer information. These include adequacy arrangements or standard contractual clauses where applicable. Their notices explain how to obtain further information about the relevant safeguards.

Children and younger players

Red Blue Tap is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not ask players to enter a birth date or verify their ages. The absence of an account does not mean that advertising services cannot process information associated with a younger player's device.

Parents or guardians with questions about a child's use of the game, or information a child may have sent to KiteMosaic, can contact us. We will consider the request, help with the available controls, and remove information held by us where required by applicable law. The advertising and privacy controls described above should be reviewed with a parent or guardian where appropriate.

Legal grounds and your rights

Where UK or European data protection law applies, the purpose and legal ground depend on the processing. To the extent that local game records and reminder timing constitute personal data, KiteMosaic relies on its legitimate interests in providing a functioning game, remembering your choices and making permitted reminders useful. This processing is limited to activity within Red Blue Tap and keeps those records on your device.

For personal information in support correspondence and relevant developer reports, our legitimate interests are answering players, resolving problems, understanding the game's operation and protecting the service. We take account of your rights when relying on these interests. Processing necessary to respond to a statutory privacy request or meet another legal duty is based on that obligation.

Where consent is required for advertising, storage or a particular use of information, the applicable privacy choices are used to obtain it. The providers' notices and consent information describe their purposes and legal grounds. A legitimate-interest ground does not replace consent where the law requires consent.

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may be entitled to access or obtain a copy of personal information, correct it, request deletion, restrict its use, receive portable information, withdraw consent, or exercise rights concerning targeted advertising or sharing.

Right to object: where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may object for reasons relating to your circumstances. Contact KiteMosaic to exercise rights over information we hold. We will respond within the period required by the applicable law and may need proportionate information to verify or locate your request. If an exception applies, we will explain it.

For data held only on your device, use the local controls described above. For information held independently by Google or another provider, its privacy controls and contact channels may be needed. You can still contact us for help understanding which route applies.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority. UK users can contact the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO); EEA users can contact the authority in their country. You do not have to contact KiteMosaic first to exercise that right.

KiteMosaic does not make automated decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects. The local reminder feature only chooses a possible notification time; advertising personalisation, where permitted, is described in Google's information.
